How this protects your data

Audio and transcripts never leave this computer. There is no cloud service, no account, no telemetry, and no third-party asset loading in this interface. Transcription runs locally with OpenAI's Whisper models. Once your model is downloaded you can engage the offline lock in AI Settings, which blocks all outbound network access at the process level.
