## Bundled data

### SCOWL — English spelling dictionary

`app/data/dictionary/en_US.txt.gz` is generated from the SCOWL-derived
Hunspell dictionaries for American and British English, expanded through their
affix rules by `assets/dictionary/build_dictionary.py`. Both varieties are
merged so that neither "program" nor "programme" is wrongly flagged.

- Source: <https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries> (`en`, `en-GB`), which

## Model weights

Whisper model weights are downloaded on demand from the Hugging Face Hub and
are **not** included in this repository. They are redistributed by their
publishers — Systran, mobiuslabsgmbh and others — under their own terms.
OpenAI released the original Whisper models under the MIT licence.

## Fonts and interface assets

None are third-party. The interface uses the reader's own system font stack and
loads no external fonts, scripts, stylesheets or images. The application icon
is original work, generated by `assets/icon/make_icons.py`.
